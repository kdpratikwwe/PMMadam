# 👑 Nimishka's Birthday Extravaganza ✨

> **"जन्मदिनमिदम् अयि प्रिय सखे | शं तनोतु ते सर्वदा मुदम् ॥"**  
> An advanced, interactive, and luxurious birthday celebration web experience created especially for **Nimishka ("PM Madam")**.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

1. 🪄 **Welcome Envelope Curtain**: A royal wax-sealed envelope overlay with smooth unlock animations and celebration popper audio.
2. ⏰ **Live Celebration Dashboard**: Party mode status, live celebration ticker, vibe meter, and unlimited love counter.
3. 🎂 **Interactive 3D Birthday Cake**: Interactive candles with blowable flame physics, smoke puff particle effects, and wish lock.
4. 📸 **Polaroid Photo Vault**: Aesthetic vintage Polaroid gallery with photo upload, localStorage persistence, and fullscreen lightbox.
5. 💖 **"Why You're Special" 3D Flip Cards**: Interactive 3D flip cards highlighting her leadership, kindness, humor, and grace.
6. 🎁 **3D Gift Box Unboxing**: Tap-to-open 3D gift box with celebration coupons and party perks.
7. ✨ **Secret Golden Scratch Card**: Mobile touch-locked interactive scratch canvas revealing personalized surprise wishes.
8. 🏆 **Nimishka Trivia Quiz**: Fun, heartwarming quiz celebrating her favorite moments.
9. 💌 **Heartfelt Letter with Sanskrit Benediction**: Written with genuine warmth on realistic parchment.
10. 🎆 **Interactive Sky Fireworks Launchpad**: Tap-to-launch night sky fireworks and a choreographed grand finale spelling **"N I M I S H K A"** in the sky!
11. 🎵 **Sanskrit Birthday Song ("जन्मदिनमिदम् अयि प्रिय सखे")**:
    - Authentic melody synthesized with Indian classical Bansuri (flute) acoustics via Web Audio API.
    - Zero external audio assets required; 100% offline & instantaneous.
    - Live synchronized Sanskrit lyrics & English meaning karaoke card.
    - Automatic fallback to local MP3 audio if supplied.
